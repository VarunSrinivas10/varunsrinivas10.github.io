Projects

Research themes and active investigations.

Grain Boundary Feature Engineering

Two-stage statistical downselection (Pearson correlation + Kruskal-Wallis) to identify the minimal set of grain boundary descriptors — triple-line count, quadruple junction count, Coxeter residual, GB area, perimeter, misorientation angle, CSL flag, and curvature spread — that discriminate kinetic outcomes in equiaxed Al polycrystals.

Synchrotron DCT Statistics Microstructure

Graph Neural Networks for Grain Boundary Classification

Edge-centric message-passing GNN operating on 3-hop grain boundary subgraphs. No node attributes are used; all information lives on edges. Trained with AdamW and cosine decay to classify grain boundary outcomes (persist / appear / disappear) with cross-entropy loss and early stopping on macro-F1.

GNN PyTorch Geometric Machine Learning

3D Tomographic Visualization: MoO₃/Fe₂O₃ Reaction

MATLAB pipeline for co-registering, segmenting, and rendering pre- and post-reaction synchrotron tomography data of MoO₃/Fe₂O₃ powder compacts. Includes log-polar FFT phase correlation for registration, isosurface rendering with custom RGB color schemes, and depth-sorted transparency for overlapping phases.

MATLAB Tomography 3D Visualization

PolyProc: Microstructural Feature Extraction

Computational pipeline for extracting grain- and grain-boundary-scale geometric and crystallographic features from 3D DCT datasets. Interfaced with MTEX for misorientation calculations and used upstream of the GNN and feature engineering workflows.

Python MTEX DCT

Grain Boundary Kinetics Derivation (3D Kelvin Cell)

Analytical extension of Mattissen et al. (Acta Materialia, 2005) to three dimensions using Kelvin cell geometry, deriving corrected grain boundary velocity accounting for triple-line drag and quadruple node drag in a self-consistent framework.

Theory Grain Growth Kinetics

Nanotomography: Al–Al₃Ni Directional Solidification

Prior-constrained silhouette carving for 3D reconstruction of Al–Al₃Ni eutectic microstructures imaged at the FXI beamline (NSLS-II), targeting quantitative characterization of phase morphology in directionally solidified samples.
